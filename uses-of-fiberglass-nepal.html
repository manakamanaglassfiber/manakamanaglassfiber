Uses of Fiberglass in Nepal

Applications in Construction and Industry

Fiberglass is widely used in Nepal in various sectors including construction, agriculture, and industry.

Construction Use

Fiberglass is used in roofing sheets, panels, and structural components due to its strength and durability.

Water Storage

Fiberglass is used to manufacture strong and leak-proof water tanks.

Doors and Furniture

Fiber doors are popular because they are lightweight, durable, and resistant to moisture.

Conclusion

Fiberglass plays an important role in Nepal’s development due to its versatile applications.
