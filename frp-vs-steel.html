FRP vs Steel

FRP (Fiber Reinforced Plastic) and steel are both used in construction, but they have key differences.

Strength

Steel is strong, but FRP offers a better strength-to-weight ratio.

Durability

FRP does not rust, while steel can corrode over time.

Cost

FRP is often more cost-effective in the long term due to low maintenance.

Conclusion

FRP is becoming more popular in Nepal due to its durability and resistance to corrosion.
